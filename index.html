How a purchase unfolds

Our fee is paid in stages, only as each milestone is reached.

  1. Consultation

    We talk through your finances and goals. If you go ahead, you sign our terms of business, privacy notice, fee agreement and a letter of authority so we can speak to lenders for you.

    £250 assessment fee, non-refundable

  2. Fact find and property

    We assess your income and outgoings. You send us the property you have chosen, or we find one for you.

  3. Decision in principle

    We choose the best lender for your circumstances and obtain a decision in principle, with an illustration showing your rate, monthly payment and term.

    £2,500, first half of our fee

  4. Application and valuation

    We submit the full application. The lender's underwriters review it and instruct a surveyor to value the property.

  5. Mortgage offer and legal work

    The offer goes to you and your solicitor. Searches take around four to six weeks, then your solicitor asks you to transfer the deposit.

    £2,500, second half of our fee

  6. Completion

    The lender releases the funds, your solicitor pays the seller's, and the keys go to you or your nominated agent. If you chose letting, we find your tenants from here.

When our fee is refunded
  • If no mortgage offer is issued after the decision in principle, we refund £1,250.
  • If completion does not happen after the offer, through no fault of yours, we refund £2,500.
  • No refund is due if you change your mind after the offer, or if completion fails because facts were not disclosed.
  • Our fee does not include solicitor, valuation or lender product fees. Stamp duty may apply; see gov.uk stamp duty rates.

What to have ready

Send each document as a legible PDF by email. Anything not in English must be translated; we'll tell you which agencies your lender accepts.

Every applicant
  • Valid passport
  • Last three months' payslips, or 13 weeks if paid weekly
  • Last three months' bank statements showing salary and outgoings
  • A recent utility bill as proof of address
  • Your most recent annual salary statement
  • A bank statement proving your deposit and how it was saved
If you are self-employed or own 20% or more of a company
  • Your last two years' accounts
  • Your last two years' tax calculations from your home country's tax authority
  • Your accountant's full details
  • The date your business started, and ended if applicable
Additionally, for Islamic finance in the Gulf
  • Six months' bank statements if you receive rental income
  • A credit report from each country you have lived in over six years, dated within 30 days: AECB (UAE), SIMAH (Saudi Arabia), Ci-Net (Kuwait), Qatar Credit Bureau, Bahrain Portal
  • Tenancy agreement if you rent your home, or title deeds if you own it
  • Three months' credit card statements and annual statements for any mortgages
  • School fee invoices, if you pay school fees
  • For a limited company: three years' accounts, Companies House number and shareholdings
